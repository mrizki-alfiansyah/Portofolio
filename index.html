Validasi Posting Date GR — Menutup Temuan Audit P2PGR Posting Date Validation — Closing a P2P Audit Finding

Sudah berjalanLive and running Mei – Juni 2026May – Jun 2026 SAP MM — MIGO · Posting Date Workflow Approval HOHO Approval Workflow UAT — Uji TerimaUser Acceptance Test

Peran sayaMy role

Menerima temuan audit P2P, membuktikan celahnya di SAP, menyusun skenario UAT bersama tim IT, menguji langsung pemblokiran di MIGO, lalu mendokumentasikan alur Lock Posting Date yang kini menjadi acuan LOG HOSM (Purchasing & Procurement).I picked up the P2P audit finding, reproduced the gap in SAP, built UAT scenarios with the IT team, tested the MIGO block hands-on, and documented the Lock Posting Date workflow that now serves as the LOG HOSM (Purchasing & Procurement) reference.

SebelumnyaBefore

SAP standar masih membiarkan GR diposting dengan posting date yang mendahului tanggal PO — bahkan tanggal PR. Kronologi Procure-to-Pay menjadi tidak benar: penerimaan barang tercatat sebelum dokumen sumbernya dibuat. Celah ini muncul sebagai temuan audit P2P yang wajib ditutup.Standard SAP still allowed a GR to be posted with a posting date earlier than the PO date — even the PR date. The Procure-to-Pay chronology became untrue: goods receipts recorded before their source documents existed. This surfaced as a P2P audit finding that had to be closed.

SesudahnyaAfter

Sistem kini mengunci kronologi di dua titik: PO date tidak boleh lebih kecil dari PR create date (message ME303), dan doc/posting date GR tidak boleh lebih kecil dari PO date — posting langsung ditolak. Untuk backdate yang benar-benar diperlukan, ada jalur resmi: sistem menahan GR, memicu approval HO, baru GR diposting sesuai tanggal yang disetujui.The system now locks the chronology at two points: a PO date earlier than the PR create date is rejected (message ME303), and a GR doc/posting date earlier than the PO date is blocked outright. When a genuine backdate is unavoidable, an official route exists: the system holds the GR, triggers HO approval, and only then posts it with the approved date.

Impact setelah perbaikanImpact after improvement

  • Temuan audit P2P selesai — GR tidak bisa lagi tertanggal di luar kronologi PR → PO → GRP2P audit finding closed — GRs can no longer be dated outside the PR → PO → GR chronology
  • Backdate terkontrol — setiap pengecualian wajib melewati approval HO dengan jejak yang jelasBackdates under control — every exception must pass HO approval with a clear trail
  • Data stok & nilai akurat — tanggal transaksi mencerminkan kejadian sebenarnya di gudangAccurate stock & value data — transaction dates reflect what actually happened in the warehouse

Kontrol Revisi Harga & Diskon pada PO Ber-GRPrice & Discount Revision Control on POs with GR

Sudah berjalanLive and running Februari 2026Feb 2026 SAP MM — PO · MIGO · MIRO Kunci Revisi di SistemSystem-level Revision Lock LEAN — Eliminasi WasteWaste Elimination

Peran sayaMy role

Menelusuri akar masalah selisih nilai GR dan IR lewat PO history, lalu bersama tim purchasing & procurement menegakkan aturan main: revisi harga dan diskon hanya boleh dilakukan setelah GR di-cancel — dan mengawalnya sampai sistem menguncinya otomatis.I traced the root cause of the GR–IR value gap through PO history, then worked with the purchasing & procurement team to enforce the rule: price and discount revisions are only allowed after the GR is cancelled — and saw it through until the system locked it automatically.

SebelumnyaBefore

Harga dan diskon pada PO masih bisa direvisi kapan pun, bahkan setelah GR terposting. Akibatnya value GR yang sudah terbuku tidak lagi sinkron dengan value IR yang dihitung dari harga baru — selisih muncul, proses invoicing di MIRO menjadi bottleneck, dan tim sibuk klarifikasi ulang. Waste berupa rework dan menunggu.PO prices and discounts could be revised at any time, even after the GR was posted. The booked GR value no longer matched the IR value computed from the new price — gaps appeared, invoicing in MIRO became a bottleneck, and teams got stuck in clarification loops. Waste in the form of rework and waiting.

SesudahnyaAfter

Urutannya kini tegas: recek PO history dulu sebelum merevisi harga maupun diskon. Selama GR masih aktif, sistem menolak perubahan — pop-up “Tolong cancel GR sebelum update harga” memaksa GR di-cancel lebih dulu. Value GR dan IR kembali sinkron sehingga invoice berjalan lancar di MIRO.The sequence is now firm: check the PO history first before revising any price or discount. While a GR is open, the system rejects the change — a pop-up (“Please cancel the GR before updating the price”) forces the GR to be cancelled first. GR and IR values stay in sync, so invoicing flows smoothly in MIRO.
